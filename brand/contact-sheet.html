Claura logo concepts

Nine directions survived. Dropped after round one: Sentinel eye (generic icon), Halo Orb (reads as Saturn), Halo Lock (padlock clip-art), Four Hands (lifebuoy), Nest (heavy blob), Ripple Wall (reads as the letters OI), Three Fields (muddy). Each card shows the mark large, at 16 and 32 px, in one colour, on light and dark, with the outlined wordmark. The two magnified squares are real 1x rasters of the 16 px mark.

01

Staggered Aura

Aura field

Two rings whose openings never line up, so nothing passes straight through; the core sits inside.

Weak at 16 px. Three elements in 16 px: the gaps close up and it reads as a target or spiral blob.

02

Glass C

Top 3Glass C

A C of stacked glass panes: the solid front pane is the seal, the faint panes behind are the aura.

Reads at 16 px. A solid C; the faint echo panes drop out, which is fine, the C stays clear.

03

Eclipse

Ring around a dot

A containment ring that thickens away from the breach it holds.

Reads at 16 px. Ring with an off-centre dot survives; it reads as an eye or moon as much as a C.

04

Bulkhead Doors

Top 3Bulkhead hatch

Two bulkhead doors slid shut, one bolt across the seam.

Reads at 16 px. Disc, seam and bolt all hold in colour; in mono the bolt is the weakest part.

05

Cradle

RecommendedShield-orb

A C-shaped aura cupping the breach in a concentric socket; the mouth is narrower than the orb, so it cannot slip out.

Reads at 16 px. A C and a dot with clear space between them: the cleanest silhouette of the set.

06

Closing Spiral

Aura that closes

One stroke winding in until it holds a single point.

Weak at 16 px. The turns merge into a smudge and the centre dot is lost.

07

Keyhole Halo

Lock-halo

A keyhole standing inside a halo that opens only at its foot.

Weak at 16 px. Holds its shape but reads as a generic lock or power icon, not Claura.

08

Enso Pearl

Calm guardian

A calm tapered ring, closed by one pearl set in its mouth.

Reads at 16 px. Ring reads; the pearl is only about 1.5 px, and the pair can echo a crescent and star.

09

Closing a

A C that closes into an a: the stem is the seal.

Weak at 16 px. Legible, but as the letters "CI" (continuous integration), the wrong message.
