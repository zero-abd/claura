# Claura: ten one-liners

Use them on the Devpost page, in captions, on the slides and at the table. Line 10 is vision: say "next" before it.

1. Your network is compromised. Your command isn't.
2. Claura is the immune system for AI data centers.
3. You don't investigate in front of the suspect.
4. Policy, not the model, decides.
5. Agents propose. Policy decides. A human approves.
6. The first responder has to be a machine, and the last word has to be a human.
7. Evidence, scope, rollback: one card, one keystroke.
8. No email, no phones, no SSO, and everyone still knows what's true.
9. The attack is a replay; the software is live.
10. Next: every breach makes the whole fleet immune.
